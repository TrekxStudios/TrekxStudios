Common questions

When will I get my Wedding Film?

Your Wedding Film is normally delivered within 6 weeks of your wedding. During peak season, or any unexpected delays, delivery can run slightly longer. If that happens, I will contact you directly, so you're never left guessing.

Do you travel for weddings?

Yes. I'm based in Southeast Pennsylvania and Southern California, and I'm happy to travel beyond those areas. Weddings that need a flight or an overnight stay include a travel fee starting at $1,200, which covers airfare, lodging, and ground transportation. Closer drives are quoted by distance. Either way, I'll give you the full number up front, with no surprises.

How does payment work?

A 30% retainer holds your date, and the remaining balance is due before coverage starts on your wedding day. All payments are made through a secure Stripe-hosted invoice, so your all payment details go to Stripe, a widely used payment processor, and are never stored by me.

Will you film the moments that matter to us?

Yes. Before every wedding, we'll have a planning call to build your shot priority list. That's where you tell me what matters most and anything special or specific to your day, like a family tradition, a surprise, or a person you want to be sure I capture. I'll plan your coverage around that list so the moments you care about are covered.

What does my coverage include?

Coverage is built around your timeline. In your pre-wedding consult, we map your day and decide where your hours are best spent. Essential packages are best used on your ceremony and the moments that matter most to you. Signature coverage can include getting ready if your schedule allows it, and Cinema is planned to run from getting ready through your send-off.
