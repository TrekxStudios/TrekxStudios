About me

Hi, I'm Ryan, a Christ-driven videographer based in Pennsylvania and SoCal. At Trekx Studios, I craft cinematic videos that tell your story. Whether it's a brand, a special event, or a lifelong memory, I handle every aspect of creating imagery that resonates.

Life may move fast, but it's a journey, a trek, and that is the base of Trekx Studios. My mission is simply to meet you where you are and showcase your story in stunning, movie-quality video.
